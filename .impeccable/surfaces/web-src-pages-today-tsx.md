# Today

Scope: the Today tab, the app's home screen. Mode: Operate (a roommate checking the day in a glance).

Audience and job: one roommate, on their phone, between classes or in the morning. Know what today holds and what needs them, act in one tap.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The day is a route. Below the briefing plate, everything that happens today (prayers, classes, events, exams, reminders, the next bus) is a stop on one vertical line in time order, with a gold "now" marker. Refuses the category default of a stack of unrelated sections that repeat each other.

OWN-WORLD: Cairo enamel street-sign plates (enamel blue #2a62a8 on navy #0e1a2b, white ring inset, enamel white type) with gold #f2b705 for now/next. Alexandria for signs, times and numbers; IBM Plex Sans Arabic for text. The line is a 4px enamel-blue rule; stops are white rings, prayer stops gold, bus stops square.

STORY: Greeting, weather now, the next twelve hours. Then the few things that need you as chips. Then the rest of today as stops. Then who's around and the week below.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Briefing plate at full width: date, Arabic greeting, temperature with sky and feels-like line, a twelve-hour strip of icon + temperature. Under it a scrolling row of "needs you" chips, then the line starting at the "now" marker with the next stops visible. Tab bar: Today, Chat, Lists, Money, Settings.

FORM: Day as a transit line; position 3 on the ordered list of seven; seed: none (unavailable).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
